All notable changes to Physgun Unbound are documented in this file.

The format is based on Keep a Changelog (https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.0.0/),
and this project adheres to Semantic Versioning (https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]

### Added
- The rotate drag (E and the mouse) turns a held entity every way, as it turns a held ship: the whole model tips and rolls about its centre. Mobs, animals, items, boats, carts, arrows, anything. What the entity's own renderer can show (its heading; an arrow's heading and pitch) goes into the entity itself, the rest is drawn by clients with the mod on top (`hold/ModelPose`, `client/ClientModelRotations`, a client mixin around `EntityRenderDispatcher`'s renderer call); the hitbox never turns. Looking up and down tips a held entity with the view too, like a ship (server `followViewPitch`, on).
- Let go or unfrozen, a turned entity rolls back upright over half a second (client `rotation.returnTicks`, 10) instead of snapping.
- A frozen entity keeps the way it was turned, through saves (its persistent data) and for every client that starts seeing it; grabbing it again, or a second player taking it over, carries on from that pose.
- Projectiles in flight are picked up: arrows, tridents, snowballs, eggs, ender pearls, potions, fireballs, wither skulls, shulker bullets, fireworks, and modded ones built on the vanilla projectile classes. Held, they stop flying (their tick is cut to `Entity.baseTick` on the server: no gravity, no acceleration, nothing hit); let go, they fly on with the throw. A stuck arrow isn't picked up.
- Anything with a body is picked up unless it is tagged, so entities from other mods (Automobility's cars) work without an entry. The tag `#physgununbound:ungrabbable` now also lists item frames, paintings, lead knots, fishing bobbers and eyes of ender; the README explains how the tag reaches modded entities.
- Client config `rotation`: `invertMouse` (false) and `returnTicks` (10).
- Gametests: 17 (was 12): the whole-model tip, the kept pose of a frozen or taken-over entity, an arrow's facing, projectiles held and thrown on, stuck arrows and bobbers refused, and a claim on motion alone not refusing.

### Changed
- The rotate drag turns things the way the mouse goes: mouse left turns the near side to the left, mouse up tips the top away. VMod's own sense had left and right swapped (its mouse-Y drag dragged the near face, its mouse-X drag turned it the other way). Fixed for ships and entities alike through one client mixin on VMod's `ClientPhysgunState.handleMouseMovement` (it negates the mouse-X angle); `rotation.invertMouse` brings VMod's sense back.
- Another mod's claim on an entity's motion alone no longer stops the physgun (the hold claims every aspect, and the mods that arbitrate `MOTION` stand down for it). Only an entity another mod scripts the whole physics of is refused: a kart in an MKW race, MKW's hazards. MKW's free-roam karts are picked up; MKW keeps driving them underneath until it yields to the hold's claim.
- A held mob's head is no longer pitched by the hold (the whole body tips instead).
- Network protocol "2": the beam carries the held entity's orientation, and a frozen-pose message was added. Server and client need the same jar.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-09-29

### Added
- VMod's physgun picks up entities as well as Valkyrien Skies ships, with its own buttons:
  - hold the left button on an entity to pick it up at the point aimed at;
  - the wheel pushes it out and pulls it in, a block a notch;
  - E and a mouse drag turn it;
  - right or middle click freezes it in place and lets go;
  - R unfreezes the frozen entity aimed at (as does grabbing it);
  - letting go keeps its speed, so a swing throws it.
- An entity in front of a ship block is picked up instead of the ship; with nothing in front, VMod picks up the ship as before.
- The hold runs VMod's own spring and damper (its server settings PCONST and DCONST), three 1/60 s steps a tick like Valkyrien Skies' physics, through the entity's collision code, up to `maxSpeed` (70 blocks a second).
- Mobs, animals, items, experience orbs, boats, minecarts (lifted off their rails), falling blocks (they don't land or expire while held), primed TNT, armor stands, entities standing on ship decks, and players. Riders come along with what they ride.
- Players are moved by their own client (the server sends the target each tick). A client without the mod is steered with vanilla motion packets. Held players aren't kicked for flying and build up no fall damage while held.
- Frozen entities keep no gravity and no AI through saves, and unfreezing restores exactly what they had.
- The physgun's beam to a held entity, drawn like VMod's beam to a held ship.
- Server config `physgun`: `enabled`, `grabPlayers`, `range`, `minDistance`, `maxDistance`, `maxSpeed`, `fallbackSpring`, `fallbackDamping`, `ungrabbable` (ids, tags, wildcards, regex).
- Entity tag `#physgununbound:ungrabbable`: the ender dragon, markers, displays, interaction boxes, area clouds, evoker fangs, lightning, and Create's contraptions, trains, seats and glue.
- Pack rules through Camafy 0.1.13:
  - held entities are claimed for every physics aspect and frozen ones for `MOTION`;
  - nothing another mod claims `MOTION` of, or the gravity mod anchors, is picked up;
  - grabbing and unfreezing ask the minigame host (`MatchPolicies.canHarm`);
  - Valkyrien Skies is reached only through Camafy;
  - resized entities move the real distance.
- If VMod's internals don't match (a later VMod), the server logs one line at start and the addon does nothing; VMod is unaffected. The VMod hook is in its own mixin config that isn't required.
- Gametests: 12 of the hold itself, and with `-Pvmod` two more against the real VMod, Valkyrien Skies, Kotlin for Forge and Architectury: VMod's own message handler driving a grab, the wheel, the rotate drag, a freeze, an unfreeze and a throw; and a pig on a real VS raft picked up (lifted, and stopped by the deck when pressed into it) while the raft is still VMod's to grab.
